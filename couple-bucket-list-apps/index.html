The 8 Best Couple Bucket List Apps in 2026 (Free and Paid)

By the Korby team · Updated October 13, 2026 · 11 min read

Most couples already have a bucket list. It’s a note on one phone, a few screenshots on the other, and a running “we should do that sometime” that rarely gets a date. The ideas aren’t the problem. Getting one of them onto the calendar is.

A good couple bucket list app fixes that: both of you add to the same list, one idea at a time gets a real date, and once you’ve done it, it’s kept somewhere you’ll find again. We compared the eight options worth considering on exactly those points. Korby is our app, so it’s first, and we’re upfront about where the others do something better.

What to look for in a couple bucket list app

  • One list, two phones. Either of you should be able to add an idea and see the other’s straight away. If only one person can edit, it’s a personal list with an audience.
  • A way from “someday” to a date. Ideas stay ideas until one gets a day. Look for dates, reminders or a countdown on the plan itself, not just on anniversaries.
  • Somewhere for the prep. Bigger plans need small steps: book the room, buy the tickets. A checklist on the plan keeps that out of your group chat.
  • Something left afterwards. Photos and a line about how it felt turn a ticked box into a memory you can scroll back through.
  • Fair pricing for two. Check whether one purchase covers both of you, and whether there’s a way to pay once.
  • No pressure mechanics, unless you want them. Streaks, scores and virtual pets motivate some couples and quietly guilt-trip others.

2. Cupla: best for couples who also want a shared calendar

Cupla is a shared calendar first: it puts both of your schedules side by side and syncs with the calendars you already use. Around that sit wishlists for date ideas, gifts and trips (you can save ideas straight from Instagram or TikTok), countdowns to key dates, a memories feed of past dates and an AI assistant you can switch off. If your real problem is finding a free evening, it covers more ground than any app here.

Best features

  • Shared calendar that syncs with Apple, Google and Outlook calendars
  • Wishlists for dates, gifts and travel, with saving from social links
  • Key dates and countdowns, including upcoming trips
  • A memories feed of past dates
  • Premium is one subscription per couple, per Cupla’s help center

Limitations

  • Wishlists are one feature among many, so the bucket list is less central than in a dedicated app
  • The free plan only shows a limited window of your partner’s items around today
  • No iPad app listed

3. Lovewick: best for date-idea inspiration

Lovewick combines a large library of date ideas with question games. You can browse 750+ free ideas, filter them by time, cost or long-distance suitability, and save them to shared wishlists to plan together, with dates on the ideas you schedule. Checked-off wishlist items feed a memory timeline, and you can hide an idea from your partner if you’re planning a surprise.

Best features

  • 1,000+ free conversation questions

Which couple bucket list app should you choose?

If the list itself is the problem, pick by what you’ll actually open:

  • You have plenty of ideas and too few dates: Korby. Every idea can carry a date, a checklist and the photos afterwards, and one purchase covers you both.
  • Your schedules are the real bottleneck: Cupla.
  • You’re short on ideas: Lovewick.
  • The photos matter most: brochi.
  • You like streaks or want it on iPad: TwoDo.
  • You want to spend nothing: Apple Reminders, or Google Keep for a mixed iPhone and Android couple.

Whichever you choose, the test is the same: within a week, at least one idea on it should have a date.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best couple bucket list app?

For turning ideas into plans you actually do, Korby: every idea can get a date, a shared checklist and photos that become a memory, and one subscription covers both partners. Cupla is better if you also need a shared calendar, Lovewick if you need date ideas, and brochi if photos matter most.

Is there a free couple bucket list app?

Withly is free (with ads), Lovewick and Cupla have free plans, and Apple Reminders and Google Keep are free shared lists. brochi and TwoDo are free downloads with paid Pro or Premium tiers, and Korby is paid after a 14-day free trial.

Do both partners have to pay?

Not with Korby: one subscription covers both of you, and your partner joins with a code. Cupla’s help center says the same for Cupla Premium. brochi, TwoDo, Lovewick and 100List don’t say in their listings.

Which couple bucket list apps work on Android?

Cupla, Lovewick and 100List are on both iPhone and Android. Korby is iPhone only, and we found no Android version of brochi, TwoDo or Withly. For a mixed couple, Google Keep is a free fallback.

What should go on a couples bucket list?

A mix of small things you could do this month, a few bigger trips or experiences you’d save for, and one or two once-in-a-lifetime dreams. Our list of 100 couples bucket list ideas is a good place to start.
