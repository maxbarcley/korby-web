The 7 Best Apps for Couples in 2026, Sorted by What You Need

By the Korby team · Updated October 14, 2026 · 9 min read

Most “best apps for couples” lists rank twenty apps as if they did the same job. They don’t. A shared calendar, a question game and a counseling app solve completely different problems, and installing five of them mostly gives you five more notifications.

So this list is sorted by problem. Pick the one that sounds most like you two, try that app for a month, and ignore the rest. Korby is our app; how we compared everything is explained below.

What to look for in a couples app

  • One clear job. Name the problem first. An app that does six things usually does your one thing less well.
  • One price for both of you. Each paid app on this list says one of its subscriptions covers both partners. Check that before you subscribe to anything else.
  • Both phones. If one of you uses Android, check the platform first. Korby, for example, is iPhone-only.
  • A way to try it. Free plans and trials let you see whether you’ll both actually open it.
  • Privacy you’re comfortable with. Especially for money apps, check what each partner can see.

2. Cupla: best for two busy schedules

Cupla puts both of your schedules side by side and syncs with Apple, Google and Outlook calendars, so you can see when you’re both free without asking. Its Date Planner lets you set a goal for how often you go out, nudges you when it’s been a while, and turns each date into a memory with a photo and notes. Shared to-dos, wishlists and countdowns to key dates sit around that.

Where it shines

  • Both calendars side by side, synced with the calendars you already use
  • Date goal, gentle nudges and Date Memories
  • Shared to-dos, grocery lists and wishlists
  • iPhone and Android; one Premium subscription per couple

Where it falls short

  • The free plan only shows a limited window of your partner’s items around today
  • Lots of features, so it does more than a couple that just wants a calendar needs

3. Paired: best for daily conversation

Paired sends you both a daily question. Each of you answers, and you see your partner’s answer once you’ve both replied, which makes it a reliable, low-effort conversation starter. It also tracks important dates. Premium adds guided journeys, expert topics such as money and love languages, and games and quizzes.

Where it shines

  • Daily questions that reveal both answers
  • Guided journeys and topics with Premium
  • By far the most-rated app here
  • iPhone and Android; one purchase gives both partners Premium, per Paired’s help center

Where it falls short

  • Most guided content is behind Premium
  • No shared planning or calendar features listed
  • Many Premium prices listed without plan names
Pricing
Free download; Premium listed at $14.99/month or $39.99–$74.99/year
Platforms
iPhone, Android

4. Lovewick: best for date ideas and question cards

Lovewick combines a library of more than 1,000 questions (one a day on the free plan) with more than 750 free date, intimacy and gesture ideas. You can filter ideas by time, cost or long-distance suitability, save them to shared wishlists, put dates on them and hide surprises from your partner. Checked-off ideas feed a memory timeline.

Where it shines

  • 750+ free date, intimacy and gesture ideas with filters, including long distance
  • 1,000+ question cards, one a day on the free plan
  • iPhone and Android; the developer says one Plus subscription covers both partners

Where it falls short

  • Questions come first; planning is secondary
  • Some long-time users were unhappy when free daily questions were cut back
  • English only

5. Lasting: best for working through recurring conflict

Lasting offers guided sessions for couples on communication, conflict, repair, trust, money and more, and lets you share your answers with your partner. A free five-session Foundations series lets you try it first; Premium unlocks the whole app for two people.

Where it shines

  • Structured sessions on the hard topics
  • Free Foundations series and conversation starters
  • Premium covers both of you; also on iPad and Android

Where it falls short

  • Built for working on problems, less for everyday fun
  • English only
  • Its latest iOS update listed is from February 2025

6. Honeydue: best for shared money

Honeydue shows balances, budgets and bills in one place, while each partner chooses how much of their own accounts to share. It sets monthly limits by category, reminds you about bills and lets you split expenses and settle up later. It’s free, with optional monthly tips.

Where it shines

  • Free, with optional tips
  • Budgets, bill reminders and expense splitting
  • You control what your partner sees

Where it falls short

  • Supports most US banks, so it’s best for US couples
  • Some reviews report bank-connection and sync problems
Pricing
Free; optional tips from $0.99 to $9.99 a month

7. Apple’s built-in apps: best if you don’t want another app

If you both use iPhone, three free apps cover a surprising amount:

  • A shared calendar in Calendar for anything with a date.
  • A shared list in Reminders or Notes for chores, groceries or a simple bucket list.
  • A shared album in Photos so you can both add pictures from the same trip.

They aren’t connected into one plan, which is the main reason dedicated apps exist. If your shared list is where plans go to wait, see our comparison of couple bucket list apps.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for couples?

It depends on the problem. Korby is best for actually doing the things you talk about, Cupla for coordinating two schedules, Paired for daily conversation, Lovewick for date ideas, Lasting for working through conflict and Honeydue for shared money.

Is there a free app for couples?

Honeydue is free with optional tips, Cupla and Lovewick have free plans, Lasting has a free starter series and Paired is a free download. If you both use iPhone, a shared calendar, Reminders list and Photos album are free and already installed. Korby is free for 14 days.

Do both partners have to pay?

Not for any paid app on this list. Korby, Cupla and Lasting Premium cover both of you with one subscription, Paired’s help center says one purchase gives both partners Premium, and Lovewick’s developer says the same for Plus.

What is the best app for long-distance couples?

Lovewick, because its date ideas can be filtered for long distance, or Paired, whose daily questions work the same whether you live together or apart.

Should couples use more than one app?

Usually not at first. Start with the app for your biggest friction point and only add another once the first has become a habit.
