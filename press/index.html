Last updated August 20, 2026

A private shared bucket list for couples: turn “we should do that” into plans you share and memories you keep.

What Korby is

Korby is an iPhone app for couples who keep sending each other places, dates, and trip ideas but never collect them in one shared place. Two partners pair their iPhones with one code. Ideas live in three states — Planned, Someday, or Dreams — and can carry dates, photos, details, and checklists. Completed plans become a shared photo timeline. Hidden plans keep surprises secret until the right moment, and more than 100 curated ideas across 10 categories help when couples need inspiration.

Korby deliberately avoids a public feed, streaks, and engagement mechanics. There are no ads. One purchase covers both partners.
